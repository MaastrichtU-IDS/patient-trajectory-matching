Custom relaxation options

Constraints are protected by default. Explicitly permit metric targets, then author up to three independent options. Changes in an option apply together; options are never combined automatically.

Gap and duration options must widen the original range. Minimum overlap must decrease and stay positive; duration bounds must stay positive.
