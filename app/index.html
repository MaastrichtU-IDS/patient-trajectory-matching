How similarity is calculated

Age-band distance is 0 for equality and 1 otherwise. Creatinine distance is the absolute difference divided by 1 mg/dL, capped at 1. Concept distance is 1 minus Jaccard overlap after the authored DrugAChild → DrugA expansion. The weighted score assigns maximum distance to missing components; at least half the weighted evidence must be observed. Lower distance means greater similarity under this profile.
