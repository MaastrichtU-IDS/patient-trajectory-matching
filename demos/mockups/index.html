Every patient here is invented. Terminology codes are illustrative and unverified. These pages explore interaction design; nothing on them is a clinical claim or a product capability.

The test dataset

The interfaces are generic; the dataset they run on here is a synthetic paediatric rare-disease cohort: 48 children across Dravet syndrome, GEFS+, febrile seizures, PCDH19, CDKL5, tuberous sclerosis, Rett syndrome and SMA, generated by demos/mockups/synthetic/generate.py. Each patient has a hidden truth timeline and the records sources would hold, with every injected defect listed: missing values and results, unit and decimal errors, transposed dates, duplicates, copied-forward text, misattributed records, approximate and conflicting onsets, late recording and late availability, reclassified variants, preterm corrected age, broader, local and inconsistent codes, and Dutch and French letters with negation, hedging, plans and family history.
