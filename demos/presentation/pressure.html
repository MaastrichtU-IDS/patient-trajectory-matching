Read the result at the right level

  1. Patients, stays and segments differ. A patient can match several strata through different treatment segments.
  2. Eligibility comes first. Missing follow-up or a lower later pressure does not remove an eligible baseline–segment pair.
  3. Review the records. Recorded segment starts are not inferred treatment-course starts. Calendar alignment is within a patient; clinical occurrence and causal effects remain unverified.
